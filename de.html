Before this goes live

For the client, in English so it is not mistaken for page copy. This block and the draft strip come off before publishing, and everything marked like this needs a real value on both language pages.

  1. Legal operator name, an email address on the new domain, and the UID if the business is on the commercial register. Required by Art. 3(1)(s) UWG.
  2. The log retention period from whichever host is chosen, for the privacy paragraph.
  3. Self-host the three typefaces, or fall back to system fonts. As it stands the pages pull fonts from Google, which sends the visitor’s IP address to Google and contradicts the line about third parties.
  4. New photography. The two shots on this page were lifted from the old site and are placeholders for this draft only. Ownership of them is unclear and they carry no licence, so they must be replaced before the site is presented as live or handed to anyone. The old logo is not used at all; the wordmark is set in type.
  5. Confirm control of the Instagram handle before it is linked anywhere permanent.
  6. Replace the Google Maps search links with the pub’s own profile short link.
  7. Add hreflang tags between the two language pages once the domain exists.
  8. Create the public Google Calendar for match nights under the landlord’s account, make it public, and put its calendar ID into the fixtures link on both pages. Until then the link goes nowhere.
  9. Have a German speaker read this page aloud once. The copy is written in Swiss spelling, with ss rather than ß, but it has not been checked by a native speaker.
